We screened 56 modeled routes and played 264 offline browser games. The native save determines which game unlocked the achievement or recorded a year. Each promoted route beat its predecessor both on eight separate validation sequences and across the two sets combined, with at least two successes in total.

The Rust Belt route visits Florida eight times, then Ohio four times. It targets 270 electoral votes while keeping the candidate in second place in Michigan, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin.

The coach shows when one year has been recorded and prioritizes tested pairings for the current year while it is still missing. The new 2016 and 2000 routes were used to complete the seven years and unlock Destiny once in Chinese and once in English.

The current MAGA route scored 0/8 on these validation sequences. Two candidates scored 1/8 apiece and neither scored in screening, so the current route stays in place. The current and shortlisted Impossible Kennedy routes both scored 0/8 on validation. These counts describe the tested sequences.
