# All 31 native achievements: individual audit

Executed 2026-11-17; report compiled 2026-11-18.

**All 31 achievements checked in 300 recorded native-browser games: 290 baseline games and 10 unsuccessful candidate replays. Completion of the audit does not mean every route passed.**

For 27 single-game objectives, follow the visible coach in five fixed fixtures (1, 3, 42, 1776, 2026): **8 unlocked in all five, 17 had mixed results, and 2 unlocked in none.** The zero-unlock objectives were `magaa` and `whatbelt`.

The two native special procedures unlocked in all three tested fixtures each. One fresh save completed 100 real 2016a games with every counter increment checked. The seven-year achievement was completed in both languages using known-success fixtures; separate year-level trials recorded only 7 qualifying totals in 35 games. These are separate kinds of evidence, not success probabilities.

All non-loopback requests were blocked. No script errors or missing resources occurred. Original algorithms/data/text integrity and all 31 eligibility checks passed. No production RNG, game values or user saves were edited.
